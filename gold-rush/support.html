Gold Rush: World Games for iPhone

Found a bug, have a question or an idea? Open an issue on GitHub and describe what happened, on which iPhone and iOS version.

Common questions

I bought the Full Games but the events are still locked. Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

There's no sound. The game follows your iPhone's silent switch. Turn silent mode off, and check that Sound is on in the game's Settings.

Can I play offline? Yes. The whole game works without a connection.

How do I start over? Settings → Reset clears your records, medals and achievements.
