Privacy Policy

Gold Rush: World Games · Last updated 7 November 2026

Gold Rush: World Games has no accounts and no servers of its own. The game itself collects no personal data. One part of the free version involves another company: ads between events, served by Google AdMob.

What the game stores, and where

The game keeps a few things on your iPhone so you can pick up where you left off:

  • Your settings: language, controls, sound and difficulty
  • The nation you last competed for
  • Your personal bests, achievements and the history of your Games
  • The Games in progress
  • Which purchases you own, and the ad schedule (how many events since the last ad)

This stays on your device. The game works offline. Deleting the app deletes this data; you can also clear your progress with Reset in the game's Settings.

Ads (Google AdMob)

From your second session, the free version shows a full-screen ad between events now and then, never during an event or a ceremony. Nothing ad-related runs during your first session.

Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects data from your device to show and measure ads: an advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), your approximate location from your IP address, which ads you saw and interacted with, and diagnostic and performance data. Before any ad, the game asks for consent where the law requires it (for example in the EU, the UK and Brazil) and shows Apple's tracking prompt. If you don't allow tracking, ads are not personalised. Ads are limited to content suitable for all ages. Google describes how it handles this data in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can change your choices any time in the game's Settings ("Ad privacy choices", where the law requires it) and in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Buying "Remove ads" or "Full Games + No Ads" turns ads off for good, and then the AdMob SDK isn't started at all.

Purchases

Full Games, Remove ads and Full Games + No Ads are in-app purchases made through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, card or Apple Account details. The game only asks the App Store which purchases you own, so it can restore them on your devices. Apple's handling of your data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

  • The game has no chat and no user-generated content.
  • Ads are limited to general-audience content.
  • The game does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Changes and contact

If a future version changes what the game stores or collects, this page will be updated and its date changed before that version is released.
