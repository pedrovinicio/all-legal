Privacy Policy

Gold Rush: World Games · Last updated 16 October 2026

Gold Rush: World Games collects no personal data. Nothing you do in the game leaves your iPhone, except purchases, which Apple handles.

What the game stores, and where

The game keeps a few things on your iPhone so you can pick up where you left off:

  • Your settings: language, controls, sound and difficulty
  • The nation you last competed for
  • Your personal bests, achievements and the history of your Games
  • The Games in progress

This stays on your device. There are no servers and no accounts, and the game works fully offline. Deleting the app deletes this data; you can also clear it with Reset in the game's Settings.

Purchases

The Full Games unlock is an in-app purchase made through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, card or Apple Account details. The game only asks the App Store whether the unlock was bought, so it can restore it on your devices. Apple's handling of your data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Tracking, ads and children

  • No tracking, analytics or advertising of any kind.
  • No third-party services or SDKs receive data from the game.
  • The game does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.
  • Because nothing is collected, the game is suitable for players of all ages.

Changes and contact

If a future version changes what the game stores, this page will be updated and its date changed before that version is released.

Questions about privacy: see the support page.
