Privacy policy

Carom Legends · Last updated 19 October 2026

Carom Legends doesn't have accounts or servers of its own. The game itself collects no personal data. Two parts of the app involve other companies: ads (Google AdMob) and online play (Apple Game Center).

What stays on your iPhone

Your settings (language, sound, aim guide, difficulty), your 8-ball and 9-ball ratings, the ad schedule and whether you bought "Remove ads" are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Ads (Google AdMob)

From your third session, the free version shows a short full-screen ad between matches now and then, never during play. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects data from your device to show and measure ads: an advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), your approximate location from your IP address, which ads you saw and interacted with, and diagnostic and performance data. Before any ad, the app asks for consent where the law requires it (for example in the EU, the UK and Brazil) and shows Apple's tracking prompt. If you don't allow tracking, ads are not personalised. Ads are limited to content suitable for all ages. Google's handling of this data is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can change your choices any time in the game's Settings ("Ad privacy choices") and in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

The one-time "Remove ads" purchase turns ads off for good, and then the AdMob SDK isn't started at all.

Online play (Apple Game Center)

Online matches, friends, invitations and leaderboards use Game Center. Apple handles your Game Center account; the game receives only your Game Center nickname to show it to your opponent, and sends your opponent your nickname, rating and the shots of the match. Your rating is posted to the Game Center leaderboards. Apple's privacy policy applies to Game Center.

Purchases

"Remove ads" is an in-app purchase processed by Apple. We never see your payment details or Apple Account; the game only asks the App Store whether the purchase was made, so it can restore it.

Children

The game has no chat and no user-generated content. Ads are limited to general-audience content.

Changes

If a future version changes what is collected, this page will be updated with a new date before that version is released.
