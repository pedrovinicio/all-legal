Common questions

How do I play online?
Online play uses Apple's Game Center. Sign in under Settings › Game Center on your iPhone, then open Online in the game and tap "Play a friend or find an opponent" to invite a Game Center friend or be matched with another player.
I bought "Remove ads" but still see ads.
Open Settings in the game and tap "Restore purchases", signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy it.
How do I move the cue ball when I have ball in hand?
A gold ring marks the cue ball. Press on it and drag it where you want it (behind the head string on the break).
What rules does the game use?
World (WPA) rules for 8-ball and 9-ball, with one change: in 8-ball you don't need to call the pocket.
There's no sound.
The game follows the iPhone's silent switch. Turn silent mode off and check that Sound is on in the game's Settings.
How do I change my ad privacy choices?
Settings in the game › "Ad privacy choices" (shown where privacy laws require it), and Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking on your iPhone.
